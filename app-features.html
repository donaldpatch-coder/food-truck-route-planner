FEATURE REVIEW / NOVEMBER 16, 2026

The food-truck tools.
Still all here.

The Street Bite retains the original Food Truck Route Planner’s owner tools and adds a public food directory. This list distinguishes available tools from the original MVP’s unfinished integrations.

Plan and run a shift

  • Dashboard: recommendations, daily opportunities and quick broadcast actions.
  • Find Locations: location/ZIP search, opportunity details, weather risk, saved/custom spots and location history. Weather needs internet; recommendations include prototype data.
  • Route Plan: weekly planning, stops, map preview and CSV export. Distances are estimates, not live turn-by-turn routing.
  • Sales Log: service timer, sales/cost entry, best sellers, competitors and notes.
  • Reports: saved sales, profit, hours and location performance.
  • Operations: applications, permits, pipeline tracking and reminders.
  • Calendar: existing planner view retained. External calendar export remains unfinished.

Market and manage a business

  • Check-in & Broadcast: location recommendation, captions, review/approval and demo broadcast. Live posting requires a configured provider.
  • Social Queue: platform captions, manual copying/posting and posting history without a paid posting subscription.
  • Truck Finder Profile: original browser-saved profile editor, menu/photo references, specials, services, event availability and booking/delivery links. File selections and AI/Toast placeholders are not a finished upload, AI conversion or POS service.
  • Shared business listing: sign in, publish/edit/hide a real public listing with a ZIP code. This is separate from the legacy browser-local profile draft.
  • Suppliers: search, partner/affiliate entries and supplier-price tracking. Submitted partner links are not independently verified.
  • POS: existing Square demo connection/import/history tools. Live Square/Toast integrations remain unfinished.

Community and account tools

  • Marketplace: original space/route listings and opportunities; prototype data and browser-local submissions remain.
  • Public Space Listings: original location submission form and saved local listings.
  • TruckNet Community: Quick Need, Locations & Events, Equipment Help and Vendor Reviews. Original community MVP, not a verified shared live forum.
  • Account: existing email authentication and profile.
  • Settings: business defaults, social-provider settings, tester feedback and data export/reset tools.
  • Help: original walkthroughs and guidance.

New for The Street Bite

  • Dining search: ZIP/city or permission-based location search, 5/10/25-mile radii, map pins and food-truck/restaurant/cafe/bakery filters.
  • 36 source-backed launch listings, reviewed ownership claims, shared business listings and diner reviews in the retained database. Signed-in reviewers get one review per business; self-reviews are blocked.
  • Live & Parked Right Now: account-owned businesses can publish time-limited public service stops. Check-ins expire within four hours; imported listings are not marked live.
  • Saved spots on this device and six indexable Lowell guide pages.
  • Installable app: home-screen icon, standalone launch and cached owner shell for supported browsers.

Internet is required for sign-in, database syncing, live weather, public listings and reviews. Local records stay on the device/browser and hostname where they were saved. Open the previous hostname’s owner app to access old local records and export them. Installation does not automatically move those records or finish unfinished integrations.
